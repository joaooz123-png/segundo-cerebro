# ATS International Conference abstracts — PF-ILD / PPF — search translation v1

Source: ATS Journals / American Journal of Respiratory and Critical Care Medicine meeting abstracts
Platform: https://www.atsjournals.org/
Target record class: ATS International Conference abstract / Meeting Abstract
Coverage: archive inception through execution date
Limits: none by year, language, disease, treatment, study design or underlying ILD

Run the following strata separately.

## Stratum A — explicit modern terminology

"progressive pulmonary fibrosis" OR
"progressive fibrosing interstitial lung disease" OR
"progressive fibrosing interstitial lung diseases" OR
"progressive-fibrosing interstitial lung disease" OR
"progressive fibrotic interstitial lung disease" OR
"progressive fibrotic interstitial lung diseases" OR
"progressive fibrosing ILD" OR
"progressive fibrotic ILD" OR
"PF-ILD" OR PFILD

## Stratum B — context-bound acronym/phenotype recall

(PPF AND ("interstitial lung disease" OR ILD OR fibros*)) OR
("progressive phenotype" AND ("interstitial lung disease" OR ILD)) OR
("progressive fibrotic phenotype" AND ("interstitial lung disease" OR ILD))

## Stratum C — broad concept recall

("interstitial lung disease" OR "interstitial lung diseases" OR ILD)
AND
(fibrosing OR fibrotic OR fibrosis)
AND
(progressive OR progression)

## Execution rule

Search ATS Journals and retain only records explicitly labelled as conference/meeting abstracts or carrying ATS meeting-abstract DOI/issue metadata. Do not restrict to a specific ATS assembly or ILD session. Preserve the exact query, execution date/time, displayed hit count, all exported records, and source-native evidence.

Recent ATS meeting abstracts may use DOI forms such as 10.1164/ajrccm.<year>.<volume>.Abstracts.<abstract-id>; older records can use conference-specific DOI patterns. DOI shape is useful for provenance but must not be used as an eligibility rule.
