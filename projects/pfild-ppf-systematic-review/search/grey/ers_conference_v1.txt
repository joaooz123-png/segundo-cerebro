## Execution rule

Use the ERS publication search and restrict the *content type* to Conference abstract only; this is a source-type restriction, not an eligibility restriction. Search all ERS conference abstract series exposed by the platform, including ERS Congress and Lung Science Conference. Do not restrict to ILD sessions, because PPF reports may appear in treatment, epidemiology, rheumatology, imaging, trial, economics or translational sessions.

Export or capture every result with title, authors, meeting/year, abstract identifier, DOI, URL and abstract text when available. Preserve source-native output/screenshots or HTML evidence of query + displayed count.
