# Search reporting and update addendum v1

Date: 2026-10-13  
Applies before human eligibility screening.  
Human eligibility decisions at adoption: **0**

This addendum prospectively clarifies search-reporting conduct identified during the PRISMA-S audit.

## 1. Contacts for additional evidence or clarification

Targeted contact may be used when one or more of the following occurs:

1. a potentially eligible report cannot be lawfully retrieved after reasonable library/open-access searching;
2. a conference abstract or registry record appears directly relevant but no publication or sufficient outcome information can be located;
3. study-family identity, overlapping cohorts, or registry linkage remains unresolved and could affect counting or synthesis;
4. critical numerical data required for an otherwise eligible nested analytical synthesis are missing or ambiguous;
5. an ongoing or completed registered study appears to lack public results.

Contacts may include corresponding/first authors, study investigators, information services, trial sponsors/manufacturers, or subject experts, depending on the question.

For every contact, preserve:
- target person/organization;
- reason for contact;
- date and channel;
- exact request;
- response/no-response;
- any records/data supplied;
- effect, if any, on study identification, eligibility, family linkage, extraction or synthesis.

Contacts are not used selectively based on favorable/unfavorable study results.

## 2. Search update policy

All executable **core identification sources** will be refreshed before manuscript submission.

Operational target:
- last search of core bibliographic databases/registries: **within 3 months of manuscript submission** whenever platform access permits;
- if more than 3 months elapse before submission, rerun the core searches again;
- supplementary sources that are materially date-sensitive (trial registries, conference archives, citation chasing) will also be refreshed when the final eligible-study set is known.

For each update preserve:
- old and new exact-as-run strategy when syntax changed;
- reason for any syntax/platform/source change;
- update date/time/timezone;
- incremental records retrieved;
- deduplication against previous exports;
- effect on PRISMA counts only after formal reconciliation.

## 3. Search filters

No published methodological search filter is currently used to limit retrieval by study design, age, human status, language, treatment, etiology or publication type in the main bibliographic searches.

Conference-abstract source restrictions are **source-type restrictions** used to search conference archives and are not review eligibility filters.

If a validated filter is introduced later, its source, validation citation, exact syntax, modification and rationale must be recorded as a protocol/search amendment before execution.

## 4. Prior work and strategy reuse

The master PF-ILD/PPF strategy was developed specifically for this project through:
- target terminology and historical terminology mapping;
- controlled-vocabulary mapping;
- known key studies/guidelines;
- sentinel validation;
- internal adversarial search review;
- translation to source-specific syntax.

No previously published systematic-review strategy was substantively copied as the master search.

Prior reviews or published searches may be used for **term discovery, sentinel discovery or cross-checking**. Any substantive reuse introduced later must be cited and disclosed according to PRISMA-S Item 11.

## 5. Multi-database searching

No current search is executed as a simultaneous multifile/multi-database search on one platform. If that changes, the platform and every constituent database will be documented.

## 6. Reporting principle

PRISMA-S governs **reporting completeness**. It is not used as a quality score. Search quality remains governed by the project's quality gates, sentinel validation and independent PRESS review.
