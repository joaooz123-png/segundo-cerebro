# Protocol amendments — version 5

All amendments below were made on 2026-08-02 before any human title/abstract or full-text eligibility decision.

## AMD-001 — synthesis architecture

**Change:** The primary product is defined as a systematic evidence census / evidence map, with methodologically coherent systematic reviews nested by question.

**Reason:** The intended corpus includes trials, cohorts, guidelines, reviews, editorials, letters, abstracts, registrations, books and regulatory documents. These cannot validly be pooled or appraised as one homogeneous systematic review.

**Impact:** No eligibility decision is changed because formal human screening has not begun.

## AMD-002 — suspend completeness claim for PubMed v1

**Change:** PubMed v1 is retained as a raw candidate retrieval set, not a definitive corpus.

**Reason:** Ten of 62 sentinel references with a PMID were not retrieved. The v1 query also generated substantial historical and semantic noise.

**Impact:** A replacement search may add records. The v1 PMID list and metadata remain immutable for audit.

## AMD-003 — independent selection

**Change:** Final full-text eligibility decisions require at least two independent human reviewers and a prespecified disagreement-resolution process.

**Reason:** Cochrane MECIR C39 treats duplicate independent final inclusion decisions as mandatory.

**Impact:** Screening infrastructure must store reviewer-specific decisions rather than a single shared decision field.

## AMD-004 — independent search peer review

**Change:** The final MEDLINE/PubMed strategy and translations to other databases require independent PRESS review before the search gate is passed.

**Reason:** Internal self-review cannot independently validate the search.

**Impact:** Search development may continue, but no completeness claim is permitted until the review is documented and corrections are resolved.
